🎯 How to Play

  1. Choose your opponent: play against the AI bot or a second player sharing your device.
  2. Choose your level: Easy uses numbers from 1–50. Medium uses numbers from 1–100 and costs 100 coins to unlock.
  3. Guess the secret number: if your guess is wrong, the game tells you whether to guess higher or lower.
  4. Watch the clues and cooldown: a difference greater than 20 gives a “WAY TOO HIGH!” or “WAY TOO LOW!” hint. After your wrong guess, the AI waits 0.5 seconds before guessing.
  5. Earn and spend coins: win against the AI to earn coins. If the AI wins, you lose 40% of your current coins, rounded down.
